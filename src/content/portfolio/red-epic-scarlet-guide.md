User guide for the EPIC and SCARLET professional camera systems. It covers the camera firmware and the physical installation and removal of key components, such as power and media modules. It also includes mechanical drawings, pinouts for input and output connectors, and technical specifications.

Published as HTML in multiple languages, with each language also available as a PDF.
